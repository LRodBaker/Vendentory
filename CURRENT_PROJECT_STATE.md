## Governing Design Criteria
The eight First Principles of Builder's Workshop govern all future Console design decisions. See `BW-MSN-001_Completion_Report.md`.

1. Optimize the executive, not the artifact.
2. Never force two difficult things at once.
3. Offer by default; rank on demand.
4. Do, don't say.
5. Uncertain out loud; never confidently wrong.
6. Feed the builders, don't become one.
7. Thinking about Builder's Workshop belongs in Builder's Workshop.
8. Propose withholding; never dictate it.
